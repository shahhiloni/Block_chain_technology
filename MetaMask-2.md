## What is MetaMask?
- MetaMask is a digital wallet (browser extension + mobile app).
- It lets you store, send, and receive Ethereum (ETH) & ERC-20 tokens, plus interact with DApps (Decentralized Apps).
- It acts like a bridge between you and the Ethereum blockchain.

## Features of MetaMask?
- Create Ethereum accounts (public/private keys).
- Send/receive ETH & tokens.
- Connect to DApps like Uniswap, OpenSea, etc.
- Works on Ethereum mainnet and testnets.

## How to Play with Bitcoin 
** Note: MetaMask doesn’t support Bitcoin directly (it’s for Ethereum and compatible chains).
But here’s how you can play with Bitcoin:

step:1: Get a Bitcoin Wallet
- Options: Electrum, Trust Wallet, Exodus, Coinbase Wallet.
- This wallet gives you a Bitcoin address (starts with 1 or 3).

step:2: Get Test Bitcoin (Free)
- Go to a Bitcoin Testnet Faucet (they give free test BTC).
- Example: https://testnet-faucet.mempool.co/

step:3: Send/Receive BTC
- Copy your Bitcoin testnet address from the wallet.
- Use the faucet to receive free testnet coins.
- Try sending between your own addresses → you’ll see how BTC transactions confirm.

## How to Play with Ethereum (Using MetaMask)
step:1: Install MetaMask
- Add Chrome extension or mobile app.
- Create a new wallet → save the 12-word Secret Recovery Phrase (very important).

step:2: Choose a Network
- Default = Ethereum Mainnet (real ETH, costs money).
- For practice → switch to Ethereum Testnets (free fake ETH).
Example: Sepolia Testnet.

step:3: Get Test ETH (Free)
- Use a testnet faucet like: https://sepoliafaucet.com/
- Paste your MetaMask testnet address → receive free ETH.

step:4: Send/Receive ETH
- Try sending ETH from one account to another.
- You’ll see gas fees in action.

step:5: Interact with DApps
- Visit a DApp like Uniswap (testnet version).
- Connect MetaMask.
- Try swapping fake ETH for test tokens.

Note: 
1. MetaMask = Ethereum wallet + gateway to DApps.
2. To play with Bitcoin → use a Bitcoin wallet + testnet BTC.
3. To play with Ethereum → use MetaMask + testnet ETH.
