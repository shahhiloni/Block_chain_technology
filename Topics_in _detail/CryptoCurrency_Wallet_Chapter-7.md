-------------------------------- CryptoCurrency-Wallet ---------------------------------------

- A cryptocurrency wallet is a digital tool that allows you to store, send, and receive cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc.

- Actually, the coins are not stored inside the wallet.
- Instead, the wallet stores your private keys (secret password) and public keys (like your account number).
- With these keys, you can access your funds on the blockchain.

## Types of Crypto Wallets
1. Hot Wallets (Online wallets)
- Connected to the internet.
- Easy to access but less secure (risk of hacking).
Examples:

1. Mobile Wallets → Trust Wallet, MetaMask
2. Web Wallets → Binance Wallet, Coinbase Wallet
3. Desktop Wallets → Electrum, Exodus

2. Cold Wallets (Offline wallets)
- Not connected to the internet.
- Very secure, used for long-term holding (HODLing).

Examples:

1. Hardware Wallets → Ledger Nano S, Trezor
2. Paper Wallets → Your private key printed on paper
