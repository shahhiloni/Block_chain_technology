---------------------------------------- MetaMask --------------------------------------
1. What is MetaMask?
- MetaMask is a cryptocurrency wallet and gateway to blockchain applications (dApps).
- MetaMask is a digital wallet (browser extension + mobile app).
- It lets you store, send, and receive Ethereum (ETH) & ERC-20 tokens, plus interact with DApps (Decentralized Apps).
- It acts like a bridge between you and the Ethereum blockchain.
- It allows users to:

1. Store and manage cryptocurrencies (mainly Ethereum and ERC-20 tokens).
2. Interact with decentralized applications (dApps) directly from a browser or mobile app.
3. Connect to blockchain networks without running a full Ethereum node.

- It’s basically your bridge between the web browser and blockchain.

2. History of MetaMask
- Launched: 2016
- Developer: ConsenSys (a blockchain company founded by Joseph Lubin, Ethereum co-founder).
- Initially, it was only a browser extension (for Chrome and Firefox).
- Later, mobile apps were released for iOS and Android.
- Today, it is one of the most popular wallets in the Ethereum ecosystem.

3. Features of MetaMask
- 1. Crypto Wallet – Store ETH, ERC-20 tokens, and NFTs (ERC-721, ERC-1155).
- 2. Browser Extension – Interact with dApps directly (DeFi apps, NFT marketplaces, games).
- 3. Network Support – Connects to Ethereum Mainnet, Testnets, and custom networks (e.g., Binance Smart Chain, Polygon).
- 4. Private Keys – Stored securely in the browser or mobile device (controlled by you, not any company).
- 5. Swaps – Built-in token swapping feature.
- 6. Non-custodial – You own your keys → you own your funds.

4. How MetaMask Works
- 1. Install the extension or app.
- 2. Create a wallet → you’ll get a 12-word secret recovery phrase (seed phrase).
- 3. Add ETH or tokens to the wallet.
- 4. Connect MetaMask to a dApp (like Uniswap or OpenSea).
- 5. Sign transactions directly from the wallet → executed on blockchain.

5. Example Usage
- You want to buy an NFT on OpenSea:
1. Connect MetaMask wallet to OpenSea.
2. Pay with ETH directly via MetaMask.
3. NFT ownership is automatically transferred to your wallet.

6. Advantages of MetaMask
- Easy to use.
- Widely supported by dApps.
- Secure (private keys stored locally).
- Free to download.

7. Limitations
- Only supports Ethereum-based assets (ETH, ERC-20, NFTs) unless custom networks are added.
- If you lose your seed phrase, you lose access permanently.
- Prone to phishing attacks if users are careless.
