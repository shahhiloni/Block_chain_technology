--------------------------- Top 5 Myths of Blockchain Technology ----------------------

Myth 1️: Blockchain = Bitcoin
Reality : Bitcoin is just one application of blockchain.
Blockchain is used in FinTech, Healthcare, Supply Chain, Voting, NFTs, Banking and more.

Myth 2 : Blockchain is Illegal or Used for Crime
Reality : Blockchain is transparent & traceable. Governments and banks use it for secure & legal transactions.

Myth 3 : Blockchain Data Can Be Easily Hacked
Reality : Blockchain uses cryptography + decentralization.
Hacking one node ≠ hacking the whole network.

Myth 4 : Blockchain is Only for Tech People
Reality : Today, anyone can use blockchain via apps, wallets, UPI-like interfaces. You don’t need to be a coder!

Myth 5: Blockchain is Slow & Not Scalable
Reality : New tech like Layer-2, Solana, Polygon, Lightning Network makes blockchain fast & scalable.
